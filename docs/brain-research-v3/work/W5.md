# W5: barge-in end to end

Owns (see `COVERAGE.md`): V-3, F-013, DR-026, DR-027, DR-028, DR-030.

Starts after W4 (the playback lifecycle) and W9 (the importance score on
proactive turns) merge. Read W4's merged contract and W9's importance field
before designing anything; this spec names them by role, not by final field
name.

## Problem

- **F-013.** A barge-in by real user speech (`confirmed_user_speech`) flushes
  audio, but neither emits a terminal `OutcomeRecord` for the stopped reply
  nor truncates its history row to what was heard (`brain_agent.py`
  `_on_audio_stop` ~1194-1260). Measured: 50/50 `confirmed_barge_in`
  scenarios end with zero terminal outcomes and unheard text in history.
- **V-3.** `chat.input` is handled strictly serially: nats-py awaits each
  callback, and the handler awaits the whole turn. So "preempt the in-flight
  turn" is structurally unreachable from speech.
- **Single slot.** One superseded-reply slot. A second supersession before
  the first resolves loses track of a reply.
- **Mutation testing** kills 33/38 barge-in mutants. Five were judged
  equivalent; re-examine them.
- **New interruption cases.** The decisions add a self-initiated thought
  interrupting (DR-026) and a proactive grace window (DR-030).

## Decisions to honour (read each file)

- DR-027: word-level truncation of heard text (unchanged granularity).
- DR-028: an ordinary confirmed barge-in cuts history exactly like the
  speculative-then-confirmed path.
- DR-026: a sufficiently significant self-initiated thought may interrupt.
  Rare, gated on the significance threshold (W9's importance; DR-025's
  detector from W2 if it has landed). Never while the user is mid-utterance
  in a way that would genuinely disrupt.
- DR-030: a highly important proactive turn that the user starts talking
  over gets a short grace window, then cedes. Anything not high-importance
  cedes instantly. Define the importance cutoff and the window length, and
  justify both against DR-024's interactive budget.

## Deliverables

1. **The explicit spec**: turn ADR-003's "known and not changed" list plus
   DR-026/027/028/030 into a written state-machine spec (states, events,
   transitions, invariants) in the ADR.
2. **Two independent `hypothesis` `RuleBasedStateMachine`s**, per the plan:
   - They drive the real `BrainAgent` with a fake NATS through random
     interleavings of `chat.input`, `audio.stop` (every source and target
     kind), W4 lifecycle events (including duplicates and reordering),
     proactive turns with high and low importance, self-thought interrupts,
     and JetStream redelivery.
   - Builder W5-A writes one. A second session, W5-B, writes the other
     from the same spec, blind.
   - The reviewer diffs their invariant sets and the failures each finds
     before either merges.

   Invariants, at minimum:
   - exactly one terminal outcome per reply
   - history equals what was heard, at word level
   - no stale stop applies to the wrong turn
   - no unbounded waits
   - every superseded reply is accounted for
   - the grace window never exceeds its bound
   - a low-importance proactive turn always cedes instantly
3. **Fixes**:
   - DR-028's history cut and terminal outcome on ordinary barge-in, using
     W4's heard offset
   - V-3: concurrent `chat.input`, so preemption is reachable. Ordering and
     backpressure must stay correct; say how.
   - the single superseded slot
   - DR-026 and DR-030
4. **Mutation re-run**: `scripts/barge_in_mutations.py` over the changed
   code. Kill count reported. Each surviving mutant is killed by a new test,
   or argued equivalent in writing, including a fresh look at the five old
   ones.
5. **Barge-in suite**: add families for self-thought interrupt and proactive
   grace (high and low importance). Keep every existing family and check.

## Acceptance (mechanical)

- Barge-in suite, 50 scenarios per family, all families:
  - every claimed invariant at 0 violations
  - `confirmed_barge_in` unclaimed history mismatch and missing terminal
    outcome at 0 (V2: 100%)
  - `replies_unresolved_without_completion` at 0 (with W4)
- Both state machines run 1,000+ examples each with no failure. Their
  invariant sets are reconciled, and any invariant only one found is in the
  merged machine.
- The mutation kill count is not lower than before on the same mutant set,
  and every survivor is explained.
- Time-to-first-audio under a barge-in is within DR-024.
- `tests/test_brainbench_gates.py` passes. Barge-in bands only improve.
- ADR `docs/brain-research-v3/adr/ADR-W5-bargein.md`, register status for
  V-3, and the F-013 status update.
