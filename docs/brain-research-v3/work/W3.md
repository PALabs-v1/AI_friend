# W3: trust and relationship model (tournament)

Owns (see `COVERAGE.md`): A-3, F-010, DR-012, DR-013, DR-014, DR-015.
Honours DR-003 (single relationship) and DR-032 (affect never overrides
safety).

## Problem

Trust is a turn counter (F-010):
- **Flat increments.** `update_from_appraisal` (`agent_state.py` ~1342-1355)
  adds `delta * NA` to integrity and `delta * (0.6G + 0.4R)` to competence on
  every turn. `G` is the agent's own mood (A-1), so a complaint, an argument
  and a thank-you raise trust by the same amount.
- **Saturation.** Integrity pins at 1.0 after turn 6 and competence after
  turn 13, identically for every persona, and trust then goes deaf: 97% of
  turns ceiling-masked over a month. On the hostile turns that land before
  saturation, trust rose every time.
- **Unused evidence model.** `PersonModel`'s evidence-based
  success/failure/rupture/repair rule exists and has no caller.
- **Overwrite hazard.** `_sync_active_person_trust_locked` would silently
  clobber appraisal-driven trust if the two were wired carelessly.

## Decisions to honour (read each file)

- DR-012: benevolence, competence and integrity stay genuinely separate. Stop
  averaging them into one value wherever that loses information. A summary
  scalar may exist for consumers that need one, but decisions read the
  components.
- DR-013: hostility means a sharp drop and slow recovery. Wire `PersonModel`'s
  rupture/repair rule.
- DR-014: competence comes from outcome evidence (did the agent actually help,
  was it thanked or corrected); benevolence from emotional tenor.
- DR-015: trust feeds relationship sentiment as a slow rolling summary.
  Distinct but connected. W2 builds the relationship-sentiment layer (DR-010);
  W3 exposes the rolling trust summary it will read.
- DR-003: one relationship this cycle. The per-person machinery may be used,
  but multi-person is not in scope.

## Where trust evidence comes from

A-1 means today's appraisal inputs carry no information about the user's
words. W3 must not wait for W2. It needs a signal about how the user treated
the agent and whether the agent helped, available in `architecture_only`
mode, where the trust suite runs.

Each design states its evidence source, and must work with both:
- the deterministic signals production already has (the heuristic intent
  classifier's labels, explicit feedback, correction markers, boundary
  words)
- W2's future user-valence estimate, as a pluggable input

Using lifesim's hidden annotations as the signal is leakage and
disqualifies a design. Lifesim annotations are for scoring only.

## Frozen rubric (the critic judges against this; builders cannot change it)

Model-free affect sim, `python -m evals.cognitive affect --turns 200`:
- hostile-script trust <= 0.4 (V2: ~0.83 after 60 hostile turns)
- recovery demonstrated after hostility ends
- no component reaches its ceiling within 100 positive turns
- reliability and warmth separated (both move trust, differently)
