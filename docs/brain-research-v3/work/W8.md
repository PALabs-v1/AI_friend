# W8: metacognition (freshness, outages, abstention)

Owns (see `COVERAGE.md`): M-8, M-10, M-13, M-14, F-014.2, F-014.3, F-015,
C1-1, X-2.

Starts after W1 merges. Both change `MemoryStore`'s search path, and W8's
contradiction surfacing reads the contradiction records W1 creates.

## Problem

- **M-10 / F-015.** `surfaced_memories` is never cleared between turns. On a
  real model, once surfacing runs, per-turn retrieval ran on 1.6% of turns
  (1/62), and 80% of the memories in context (237/295) were stale
  carry-overs from earlier turns.
- **M-8.** Surfacing-agent retrieval failures never reach the brain:
  `SurfacedMemory` has no outage field, and `_on_memory_surfaced` drops
  content-free items. Measured: 7 injected outages, 0 visible.
- **F-014.3.** `MemoryStore.last_search_error` is one shared field that the
  next successful search clears, so it is not a durable outage signal.
- **C1-1.** `search_memories(threshold=...)` is ignored under the hybrid
  policy. Hybrid scores are pool-relative, so an absolute cutoff needs a
  calibrated relevance floor, not the raw score.
- **M-13.** `learning.py` ignores `add_memory`'s return, so the subconscious
  marks episodes consolidated even when the summary failed to store.
- **M-14 / X-2.** The Qdrant wing filter at query time (R-10 claims it was
  fixed; verify on live Qdrant), and pgvector's post-filter shrinking the
  candidate pool below the requested size.
- **F-014.2.** The surfacing agent's throttles read `time.time()`, not
  `app.clock` (`surfacing_agent.py` ~111, ~187).

## Deliverables

1. **Per-turn freshness (M-10).** Surfaced memories carry the turn they were
   surfaced for and an age. The brain decides per turn whether surfaced
   context is still relevant, and runs its own retrieval when it is not.
   Stale carry-overs expire by turn distance and relevance to the current
   input, not by never.
2. **Outage propagation (M-8, F-014.3).**
   - An `outage` / `degraded` field on the `memory.surfaced` contract, in
     every language that has the contract. Keep fixture parity.
   - A durable per-store degradation state with timestamps, not a field the
     next success clears.
   - The brain sees degraded retrieval and can say it cannot remember right
     now, rather than confabulating.
3. **Abstention (C1-1).** A relevance floor calibrated on real embeddings
   (Phase 4a's cached vectors, and P8's corpus if it has landed), exposed as
   the meaning of `threshold` under hybrid. Report the calibration curve.
   Wire abstention into the answer path, so an unanswerable question gets
   "I don't know" instead of the nearest memory.
4. **M-13**: consolidation marks an episode consolidated only when its
   summary was actually stored. A failed store is retried, or kept pending
   with its reason (this ties to W7's F-007; coordinate, don't duplicate).
5. **M-14 / X-2**: verify both on live Qdrant and pgvector (P8's stack).
   Fix what reproduces, and record what doesn't with evidence.
6. **F-014.2**: surfacing throttles use `app.clock`, so simulated replays
   pace them. Remove the metacognition suite's bypass once it is no longer
   needed, and keep its measurement.
7. **Contradiction surfacing.** When W1 has recorded that a new statement
   contradicts an old one, the brain can notice and say so ("you said X
   before"), gated so it does not nag. The metacognition suite's
   `contradiction_recording` becomes measurable; it was n = 0 at V2 because
   the persona's week had no contradictions, so pick dev personas with a
   high contradiction rate.

contradictions, `1m` (the reviewer runs it through the runner):

| stale carry-over share of memories in context | 80% | < 20% |
| injected outages visible to the brain | 0/7 | 7/7 |
| AUROC (answerable vs unanswerable) | 0.79 | not lower |
