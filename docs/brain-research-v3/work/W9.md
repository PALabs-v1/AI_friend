## Problem

- **F-011, the flood.** Once the user is idle 2 h, V2 reaches out every
  tick, 60 per idle hour, instead of at most once per cooldown. Three
  reasonable pieces combine:
  1. The brain's `handle_system_tick` ends in a `state.broadcast` carrying
     its own `last_proactive_attempt`.
  2. `mark_proactive_attempt` runs only in the subconscious and does not bump
     `revision`, so the next broadcast overwrites it (`apply_external_state`).
  3. `brain_agent.py` (~971-973) does not count the agent's own outreach as
     interaction.

  Measured in one simulated week: 6,836 initiations, 35% at night, 0 useful.
- **Gating.** Initiation gates on a flat timer plus a coin flip, before any
  thought exists. The decisions want importance-weighted,
  context-aware initiation with its own category for self-initiated
  thought, and diminishing returns for ignored thoughts.
- **Dead code.** `GoalRecord` / `review_due_goals` (`cognitive/goals.py`) have
  no production caller. `BackgroundScheduler` (`cognitive/background_scheduler.py`)
  is constructed in `core.py` and handed to the pipeline, but nothing ever
  enqueues work.

## Decisions to honour (read each file)

- DR-016: idle/cooldown stay a floor; importance decides urgency. The
  subconscious's existing thought-generation LLM call is the natural place to
  also score importance (no second LLM call).
- DR-017: each raise-and-ignore lowers re-raise probability, reaching a real
  zero (a floor, not an asymptote). Wire `GoalRecord`/`review_due_goals` for
  this, with outcomes (acted on / dismissed / ignored).
- DR-018: time-of-day and recent-activity gating, heuristic first. The
  persona's sleep window is the obvious first signal. Lifesim knows each
  persona's rhythm; the brain must learn or infer it from the user's own turn
  times, never read it from lifesim.
- DR-019: a self-directed thought category with a stricter gate than
  useful-to-user.
- DR-024: background never delays a foreground turn.
- DR-002: an autonomous mind, not an assistant. Self-initiated thought is
  legitimate, just rarer.
- DR-032: nothing here may suppress a safety or boundary response.

## Deliverables

1. **Fix the flood** (V-4 / F-011) at the root, not by special-casing the
   benchmark. Choose among the F-011 candidates (max-merge of
   `last_proactive_attempt`, brain records the attempt when it processes a
   subconscious-sourced turn, or the cooldown check moves to the brain) and
   justify the choice in the ADR. Regression tests reproduce F-011's
   three-piece mechanism with the real `StateService` pair and the brain
   tick handler, as the proactive suite's `broadcast` arm does.
2. **Importance** (DR-016):
   - Every candidate thought carries `importance` in [0, 1] and a `category`
     (`useful_to_user` | `self_directed`).
   - With a model: from the existing thought-generation call's structured
     output, validated and clamped. An unparsable or missing score falls
     back to the deterministic estimate; it never silently counts as high.
   - Without one: a deterministic estimate from structured signals the
     brain actually has (due and upcoming goals and commitments, unresolved
     thoughts' history).
   - Importance scales eligibility above the idle/cooldown floor.
   - Expose importance on the outgoing proactive turn so W5 can implement
     DR-026 and DR-030. Contract field, both languages if it crosses to
     Rust.
3. **Diminishing returns** (DR-017) via `GoalRecord` / `review_due_goals`:
   raise history, outcomes, decay to exactly zero after a bounded number of
   ignores. The decay constants go in `Config`, with the reasoning.
4. **Context gating** (DR-018): a quiet-hours / activity heuristic learned
   from the user's own interaction times (with a sensible default before
   there is history), plus a per-category threshold (DR-019: self-directed
   stricter).
5. **`BackgroundScheduler`**: wire it to real work (reflection or
   consolidation jobs that exist today) or delete it and its pipeline hooks.
   Decide from the code and DR-024, and justify it in the ADR. Either way
   add the DR-024 starvation test: a foreground turn under concurrent
   background consolidation stays within its budget (measure it, and assert
   against a stated number).
6. **BrainBench**:
   - Extend the proactive suite to run `llm_augmented` too, so the real
     importance path is measured. Keep every existing check and the
     architecture_only mode.
   - Add metrics for importance distribution, category split, per-category
     useful rate, re-raise decay (raises per ignored thought), and quiet-hour
     initiations.
   - Record everything in the suite's scoring functions.
7. ADR `docs/brain-research-v3/adr/ADR-W9-proactive.md` (one page), a
   `findings.md` status update for F-011, and a register status update for
   V-4.

## Acceptance (mechanical)

panel at `1w` and `1m`, `architecture_only`:
- `cooldown_violations` = 0 and `last_proactive_attempt_resets` = 0.
- Median initiations per idle hour past threshold <= 1 / (cooldown in hours).
- `annoyance_count` per simulated day at or below the `none`-arm V2 control
  (21.8/day on the gate slice), and `useful_rate` above V2's.
- `night_fraction` below V2's (0.365 on the gate slice).
- Re-raise count of a repeatedly ignored thought reaches 0 within the
  configured bound.

if the builder's sandbox cannot): importance distribution not degenerate
(not all equal), `self_directed` share below `useful_to_user`'s.
