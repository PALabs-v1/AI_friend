Source URLs below were fetched by the research verification pass; publisher/author/primary sources are preferred. `VERIFIED` means bibliographic identity and the claim/formula stated here were checked in the cited source. `PARTLY` means the source identity and broad finding were checked but the exact equation, parameter, page, or full text was not. `NOT VERIFIED` means the citation/claim as written is unsupported or misattributed. Local fixtures marked as such are not presented as published worked examples.
