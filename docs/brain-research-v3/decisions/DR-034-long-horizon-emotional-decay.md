# DR-034: Even emotionally-protected memories eventually fade, just very slowly

**Round**: 10 (long-horizon, final)
**Date**: 2026-09-24
**Question**: Does emotional protection (DR-007) mean near-permanence, or a much slower decay that still eventually approaches unreachability?

**Decision**: fades eventually, just very slowly. A much longer half-life than trivia, but a genuinely never-revisited significant memory can still approach practical unreachability after enough decades — matching how even real emotional memories fade with enough time and disuse.

**Consequence for W1/W7**: DR-007's two-regime decay curve (fast for trivia, slow for emotionally significant) needs both regimes to converge toward the same "practically unreachable" floor eventually — not a permanent floor for the slow regime. This matters concretely for the 10-20 year lifesim horizons in `06-benchmark-plan.md`: the benchmark should include probes that test whether a decades-old, never-revisited significant memory has in fact become hard to retrieve, not assume it stays retrievable forever by construction.
