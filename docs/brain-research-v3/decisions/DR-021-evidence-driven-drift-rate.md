# DR-021: Evolution rate is evidence-driven, no explicit cap

**Round**: 6 (learning/evolution)
**Date**: 2026-09-24
**Question**: How fast should adaptive traits/speaking style drift, once applied per DR-020?

**Decision**: no explicit rate limit. The 0.8 confidence bar (DR-023) is the only throttle — if genuinely high-confidence reflections happen to cluster, the personality can shift at whatever pace that evidence actually supports, consistent with DR-020's "like a human" framing (a person's personality can shift quickly during an intense period of life, not on a fixed schedule).

**Consequence for W11**: no additional rate-limiting logic needed beyond what the confidence threshold and the existing 5-trait cap already provide. This simplifies W11's scope relative to what a rate-limited design would have required — one less mechanism to build and tune.
