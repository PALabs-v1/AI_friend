### Current findings

1. **HIGH — `03-realism-eval.md` §§Tier 2, lines 97–128; `tools/power_pairwise.py`. The claimed 80% power does not cover the proposed clustered analysis.** I independently reproduced the script’s independent-judgment calculation: at n=90 and true preference .65, joint-rule power is **0.812288**; at n=200 and true preference .60, it is **0.530662**. The latter matches the reported 53.1%. But the eval then proposes a rater random effect and converts effective n to raw judgments using a design effect. The script’s binomial power calculation is still for independent judgments; it does not simulate or calculate power for the random-effects model, nor account for repeated personas or transcript excerpts. The “80% at .65” statement therefore does not establish 80% power for the analysis the eval proposes. **Fix:** define the analysis unit and all clustering, then use a pilot-based power simulation for the specified mixed model. Until then, label the current figures as independent-judgment illustrations.

2. **HIGH — `03-realism-eval.md` §§Tier 0–4 and “Before freezing.” The study cannot be preregistered or run as written.** The document itself lists twelve groups of unresolved items. These include D1–D4; definitions and denominators for continuity and repair; drive and inner-life ablations and thresholds; identity thresholds at the proposed horizons; Tier 1 scoring and calibration; Tier 2 items, excerpt length, sampling, raters, and ICC pilot; Tier 3 measures, analysis, and pass criteria; Tier 4 scoring and harm margins; and the full analysis plan, including missing-data handling. Other pass language also lacks operational rules: “above chance,” “scores lower,” “appropriate,” and “must exceed the ablated arm” have no effect-size threshold or specified scoring procedure. **Fix:** resolve each decision and specify stimuli, measures, sampling, analysis, denominators, thresholds, and stopping rules before treating this as an acceptance test.

With one to five users, the document correctly calls this case studies, but does not say how those cases would support the singular target claim. **Fix:** specify the Tier 3 estimand and pass criteria, crossover and carryover handling, and exactly what claim a small case series can support.
