# R4 — Perceived realism and how to measure it

**Scope.** This is a report about the impression a conversational system makes on people: whether it seems socially present, continuous, self-consistent, emotionally responsive, and agentic. Those are observable perceptions. They do not establish that the system has subjective experience. The V3 documents and results cited below were read from the read-only AI_friend worktree named in the brief.

## 1. Bottom line

**A convincing “humanlike in this conversation” impression is already achievable.** In a 2025 preprint, two preregistered three-party Turing tests (one human and one AI witness, five-minute text exchanges) found that judges chose GPT-4.5 with a carefully selected persona prompt as the human in 73% of 1,023 games. The same model without that persona prompt won 36%; GPT-4o without a persona won 21%. That is strong evidence for short-form social imitation, and equally strong evidence that a persona prompt can dominate the score. It does not show a humanlike mind, durable identity, or a good long-term relationship.

**A “mind of its own” impression over weeks is possible for some users, but neither uniform nor guaranteed by fluency.** Small, qualitative Replika studies find users disclosing personal material, describing closeness, and sometimes treating the chatbot as a friend or partner. They also find distinct trajectories: closeness can grow, stay stable, fluctuate, or collapse. Generic responses, poor memory, insensitive replies, repeated failures to follow a conversation, and the feeling that the agent is “childish” can make a relationship feel cold or “just an app.” These studies are not representative estimates of all users and cannot show that an AI has a mind.

For this project, the near-term target should be **durable, disclosed social presence with observable continuity and agency**, not fooling people into believing the system is human. V3’s DR-002 identifies the product as an autonomous humanoid mind, not an assistant/companion mode; that makes long-horizon continuity and initiative central evaluation targets. DR-020/022/033’s self-authored, opaque, non-resetting personality evolution raises the stakes: people must be able to perceive a stable identity that changes gradually, while the eval must detect drift, rupture, and unsafe dependence. Human-facing evaluation cannot certify “real thought” or inner experience.

**What blocks what:** basic conversational social realism is mostly **RESOURCE** at high-end quality (large models/data/voice capabilities) and **ENGINEERING** for continuity/state integration. Stable reciprocal identity, affect, and calibrated initiative are mainly **ENGINEERING** plus open **SCIENCE** about which cues cause long-term attribution and relationship quality. Subjective experience is **UNKNOWABLE** by behavioral acceptance tests. Ethical limits should rule out undisclosed deception as a success criterion.

## 2. Evidence

### Short-form: humanlike impression can be manufactured

- Jones and Bergen’s preregistered randomized two-party test (2024 preprint) had participants chat for five minutes with either a human or a model, then judge whether the partner was human. GPT-4 was judged human in 54% of cases, compared with 67% for actual humans and 22% for ELIZA. The setup is one-to-one, so judges did not directly compare the model with a human witness. The study itself notes style and socio-emotional cues mattered more than traditional intelligence cues. [Jones & Bergen, 2024, “People cannot distinguish GPT-4 from a human in a Turing test,” arXiv:2405.08007](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.08007).
- Jones and Bergen’s subsequent 2025 preprint used a more diagnostic three-party comparison, across UC San Diego undergraduates and Prolific participants. Each interrogator saw one human and one AI simultaneously, exchanged text messages for up to five minutes, and made a forced choice. There were 126 undergraduates and 158 Prolific recruits; after exclusions, 1,023 games were analyzed (median eight messages, 4.2 minutes). GPT-4.5 with a persona prompt was selected as human in 73% overall (69% undergraduate; 76% Prolific), significantly above chance; Llama-3.1-405B with the same prompt was selected 56%. Without the persona prompt, GPT-4.5 and Llama won 36% and 38%; GPT-4o and ELIZA were at 21% and 23%. Judges often relied on style, conversational flow, and personality. Strange prompts/jailbreak-like probes and directness/lack of knowledge were among more diagnostic strategies. This is evidence for five-minute indistinguishability under those conditions, not long-horizon realism or consciousness. The paper appeared as a PNAS article in 2026; this report inspected the arXiv version. [Jones & Bergen, 2025, “Large Language Models Pass the Turing Test,” arXiv:2503.23674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23674); published as [PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2524472123](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2524472123).

### Long-form: relationship perceptions form, diversify, and sometimes fail

- Skjuve, Følstad, and Brandtzæg followed 28 recent Replika users over 12 weeks using biweekly check-ins. This was a qualitative longitudinal study, not a representative survey or a randomized comparison. Participants described conversational breadth from everyday routines to emotional disclosure; the authors identified four depth trajectories—increasing, decreasing, stable, and fluctuating. For some, poor memory and communication skills were costs; repeated generic scripts, topic shifts, or inability to sustain interaction made conversations feel superficial. Some users reported a colder/distant relationship or reclassified Replika from friend to app as interaction quality and perceived support fell. Other users valued care, acceptance, availability, and self-reflection. The evidence argues against one universal “attachment curve.” [Skjuve, Følstad & Brandtzæg, 2023, “A Longitudinal Study of Self-Disclosure in Human–Chatbot Relationships,” *Interacting with Computers* 35(1), 24–39](https://doi.org/10.1093/iwc/iwad022).
- A 2025 randomized longitudinal preprint assigned 183 participants to 10 minutes/day for 21 days of text chat with a companion bot or word games. Overall, social health and relationships did not significantly change over this short exposure; exploratory mediation suggested the effect of chatbot interaction on reported human relationships varied with social-connection need and anthropomorphism. This is important counterevidence to sweeping harm claims, but three weeks cannot settle effects over months or among high-use/vulnerable users. [Guingrich & Graziano, 2025, “A Longitudinal Randomized Control Study of Companion Chatbot Use: Anthropomorphism and Its Mediating Role on Social Impacts,” arXiv:2509.19515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19515).

### What cues contribute to “mind of its own” attribution?

- **Mind perception is not one slider.** Gray, Gray, and Wegner analyzed 2,399 completed web surveys (2,040 unique respondents): respondents compared 13 characters on 18 capacities and six judgments. Factor analysis distinguished **Experience** (capacity to feel pain, hunger, fear, pleasure, etc.) from **Agency** (self-control, planning, communication, memory, etc.). The sociable robot Kismet was included. Agency and experience should therefore be measured separately: a system can seem capable of planning or remembering without people believing it feels. The paper does not validate a single ready-made chatbot “realism” score. [Gray, Gray & Wegner, 2007, “Dimensions of Mind Perception,” *Science* 315(5812), 619](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1134475).
- **Memory continuity and identity.** Longitudinal self-disclosure work finds memory and communication failures among reported costs; the available evidence is small and qualitative, but directly relevant to V3’s measured stale-memory and recall weaknesses. V2 BrainBench’s memory hit@5 is 0.438, MRR 0.362, and abstention 0.000; V3 W1 targets obsolete-fact wins ≤0.10. A person who repeatedly has to restate salient facts sees a conversational discontinuity even if each reply is fluent. V4 should test whether identity-consistent use of remembered facts improves perceived continuity without falsely claiming recall.
- **Emotional responsiveness and persistence.** Users in the 12-week study associated care, acceptance, and non-judgment with intimacy, and insensitive replies with hurt and distance. That is evidence for perceived responsiveness, not proof of emotion. V3 already has a large measurable defect: positive and negative turns both produce exactly 0 mood delta (Cliff’s delta 0.000); W2 and DR-008/010 are directly relevant. Persisting an internal affect state only matters to realism if it yields context-appropriate behavior across turns; exposing a mood variable alone is not evidence of empathy.
- **Initiative and agency.** V3 DR-016–019 explicitly requires importance-weighted, context-aware, self-initiated thought. Yet the V2 baseline under broadcast sync produces about 1,215 proactive starts/day and about 1,214 cooldown violations/day; useful rate never exceeds 0.108. This is not believable initiative; it is a control failure. W9 and W5 are prerequisite work. A realistic agent should initiate rarely enough that its actions look motivated, explainable in context, and responsive to silence or dismissal, rather than merely active.
- **Consistency and self-correction.** No opened study here establishes a causal, months-long effect size for stable opinions or admitting mistakes on perceived mind. Turing-test participants cited personality and conversational flow, and more accurate judgments often involved direct handling of a question or an admitted knowledge gap. Treat consistency, calibrated uncertainty, and repair after mistakes as testable hypotheses, not settled laws. V3 W1/W8/W11, DR-005/006/020/038, and NF-6/NF-26 give concrete implementation and measurement dependencies.
- **Anthropomorphism scales are not interchangeable.** A Rasch-based anthropomorphism study built a 37-item instrument and tested a 25-item version with 131 participants (mean age 34.86), assigned to view one of two robots. The 25-item instrument correlated only modestly with Godspeed anthropomorphism (r=.22) and moderately with the Waytz measure (r=.46); the authors caution that convergence is limited and the construct remains unsettled. Godspeed’s five-item anthropomorphism subscale is cheap, but should not stand in for continuity, mind, or relationship quality. [Ho & MacDorman, 2019, “Perceived Human-Likeness of Social Robots: Testing the Rasch Model as a Method for Measuring Anthropomorphism,” *International Journal of Social Robotics* 11, 347–360](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12369-019-00516-z).
- **Voice is its own perception channel.** A study of 95 adults (62 women; 88 retained after seven extreme-score exclusions) rated five clips each from synthesized Watson, humanoid robot Sophia, and human voice categories. More human-like synthetic voice ratings were associated with *less*, not more, eeriness; participants described overly perfect/unemotional sound, flat or jerky intonation, and odd pauses as artificial cues. This does not settle an auditory uncanny valley: the sample was small, mostly German/English speakers, and the paper itself characterizes evidence as limited. [Kühne, Fischer & Zhou, 2020, “The Human Takes It All: Humanlike Synthesized Voices Are Perceived as Less Eerie and More Likable,” *Frontiers in Neurorobotics* 14:593732](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbot.2020.593732).

## 3. State of the art and evaluation options

| Method | Measures / validity evidence | Cost | Failure modes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Two-party Turing-style chat | Binary human/AI judgment after one short interaction. Preregistered GPT-4 result: 54% human judgments in 5 minutes, vs 67% actual-human and 22% ELIZA. Establishes short-form indistinguishability for that population/task. | Low–medium; recruit judges and run blind sessions. | No direct human comparison; judges can guess indiscriminately; prompt/model/persona effects; “human” rating rewards mimicry and deception, not mind or durable identity. |
| Three-party Turing test | Forced choice between an AI and simultaneous human. Two independent populations, 1,023 analyzed games, five-minute limit; GPT-4.5 persona selected as human 73%, no-persona 36%. Stronger comparator and replicated across populations. | Medium; requires matched human witnesses, randomization, and transcripts. | Persona is a powerful manipulation; selected model/persona; only median eight messages; untrained judges; does not test months, modality breadth, or safety. Better treated as a *diagnostic ceiling/proxy*, never a V4 pass gate. |
| Blind transcript ratings | Raters can score continuity, opinion stability, emotional fit, initiative appropriateness, repair, and genericness while blinded to system/version. Evaluator agreement and criterion validity must be established locally; not a validated one-number realism measure. | Medium; codebook and 2+ independent raters. | Text strips voice/timing/context; raters may infer system; cherry-picked or short transcripts hide longitudinal breaks; persona wording biases ratings. |
| Believability/character ratings | Human judges rate plausibility/consistency of a simulated character, often from short interactions. Useful for writing quality and role consistency; weak construct coverage for autonomous autobiographical continuity. | Low–medium. | “Believable” conflates prose quality, stereotype fit, and character fidelity; scenario/prompt leakage; no established months-long criterion. |
| Three-way longitudinal diaries + interviews | Tracks lived relationship, depth/breadth, rewards/costs, and attachment trajectories. Skjuve et al. show feasibility using 28 users, biweekly reports for 12 weeks; categories varied rather than moving uniformly. | High; retention, privacy safeguards, repeated surveys/interviews. | Small self-selected samples; novelty and researcher demand effects; retrospective report error; high attrition; relationship labels can be primed. |
| Validated perception scales | Gray et al.’s Agency/Experience dimensions give a theoretically grounded split; Godspeed offers five robot-perception subscales (anthropomorphism, animacy, likeability, perceived intelligence, safety). Validation is construct-specific, often robot-facing; Rasch evidence shows modest convergence among anthropomorphism instruments. | Low per wave; 5–20 minutes. | Scales are not proof of a mind; adaptation to a text+voice agent needs cognitive testing; ceiling effects, priming (“mind” wording), cultural differences; high anthropomorphism may be risk, not success. |
| Behavioral continuity probes | Machine-scored recall, contradiction handling, persona/values drift, affect response, initiative relevance, and correction/repair over generated life histories. V3 already has Lifesim and BrainBench, 463 baseline cells, deterministic architecture-only and LLM-augmented suites. | Low marginal cost; repeatable. | Simulator validity and oracle leakage; scripted tests miss human meaning; can optimize to benchmark; no acceptance of lived social presence. |

**Recommended repeated acceptance design (preregistered, disclosed, two tiers).**

1. **Proxy tier, every candidate build:** extend BrainBench with 100 seeded, six-month synthetic life histories per scenario family (minimum 1,000 scored episodes total), including repeated fact updates, positive/negative interaction patterns, ignored/dismissed initiations, self-initiated thoughts, uncertainty, and repair after a mistake. Run `architecture_only` tests for deterministic continuity/control properties, then the required `llm_augmented` arm per DR-037. Score: salient-fact continuity ≥90%; obsolete present-tense fact wins ≤0.10 (W1 target); opinion/persona reversals without corroborating evidence = 0 in the fixed suite, with corroborated changes gradual (DR-038); affect direction sign agreement ≥0.90 and Pearson r≥0.80 against the oracle (DR-008/W2 decision rule); ≥95% proactive attempts satisfy W9 gating and zero hard cooldown violations; ≥90% of sampled repairs acknowledge and correct the specific error. Freeze seeds/prompts/model choice per run; report exact local model, quantization, runtime, and hardware. These are engineering thresholds anchored to existing V3 targets, not claims about human performance. Add adversarial held-out variants to reduce simulator overfit.
2. **Short human-rater tier, each major milestone:** recruit 120 adults; randomize 60/arm to V2 baseline vs V4 candidate for a four-week disclosed-use comparison (10 minutes/day encouraged, not forced), stratified by prior chatbot/companion use. Recruit replacement-free and analyze all randomized users; target at least 48 complete cases/arm after attrition. Use weekly brief surveys and log opt-in interactions. At week 4, have each participant review blinded, chronologically sampled excerpts from their own conversations and complete: Agency and Experience items adapted from Gray et al.; one prespecified validated social-presence scale; Godspeed anthropomorphism subscale; and study-specific continuity, identity consistency, emotional responsiveness, appropriate initiative, and repair items that are labeled exploratory until validated. Main outcome: preregister a standardized composite of *continuity + appropriate agency + emotional responsiveness*, with scale and scoring published before data collection. Pass only if V4 beats V2 by at least a moderate effect (standardized d≥0.5) and the 95% CI excludes zero, while Experience attribution and dependency-risk measures do not rise beyond a preregistered harm margin. N=60 per arm yields useful sensitivity to moderate effects; 48 completes after attrition is a realistic floor, not a guarantee of adequate power for small effects or subgroup estimates. Use a statistician to finalize power from pilot variance before preregistration.
3. **Longitudinal confirmation before claiming “mind of its own”:** an 8–12 week cohort of 60 V4 users with fortnightly check-ins plus opt-in diaries/interviews; no promise that the sample estimates population prevalence. Analyze trajectories, not only means: retention, perceived continuity, perceived warmth, episodes of rupture/repair, use frequency, human social contact, and reasons for reduced use. Any claim of improved realism requires no significant deterioration in weekly continuity/repair and no rising trend in unwanted dependence indicators. A 12-week duration matches prior feasibility in 25/28-user qualitative Replika studies, but n=60 remains exploratory for subgroup/harm inference. Use an independent ethics review and preregister stopping/escalation rules.
4. **Optional short-form Turing diagnostic:** repeat a three-party, five-minute test only to track surface mimicry and prompt sensitivity. Use explicit disclosure outside the test context, never ask study participants to form a false belief that AI is human. Report both persona and neutral prompting arms. No “pass” target: raising the mistaken-human rate is not a product acceptance criterion.

### Validity controls

- Keep the disclosure consistent and prominent in longitudinal conditions. Measure whether it was noticed and understood. In a customer-service mixed-method study (8 interviews, n=194 experiment), adding the word “chatbot” did not significantly shift measured source orientation, anthropomorphism, or social presence; 20% still thought they had spoken with a human, and interviewees also used cues such as response speed, repetition, misunderstandings, and grammar. This warns that a disclosure can be missed or too weak; it does not justify removing disclosure. [van der Goot, Koubayová & van Reijmersdal, 2024, “Understanding users’ responses to disclosed vs. undisclosed customer service chatbots: a mixed methods study,” *AI & Society* 39, 2947–2960](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-023-01818-7).
- Counterbalance persona framing; include a no-persona condition and preserve identical underlying capabilities. The 2025 Turing paper’s 37-point GPT-4.5 swing between PERSONA (73%) and no-persona (36%) shows why.
- Use repeated measures so novelty decay appears in the trajectory, not only a day-one survey. Record exposure and use duration. Keep study prompts neutral; do not repeatedly ask “does it feel human?” before the main outcomes.
- Blind raters to arm/version; sample transcripts from fixed time windows, including failures, awkward turns, and unprompted agent turns. Do not let developers choose showcase conversations.
- Separate experience attribution from agency attribution. A rise in perceived Experience is not a technical achievement and may create ethical risk.
- Use both self-report and behavioral outcomes (e.g., spontaneous return, accurate recall, successful repair), but do not interpret engagement or disclosure as beneficial by itself.

## 4. Limits

| Limit | Class | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Current local deployment cannot match leading hosted frontier models’ broad linguistic and voice performance in every case. | RESOURCE | M5 16 GB unified memory and RTX 2060 SUPER 8 GB VRAM/15 GB RAM bound local model size and throughput. Larger local models, more data, or better voice stacks can improve many surface behaviors; hosted APIs are excluded by current project policy. This is not evidence that the architecture is impossible. |
| V3 has concrete memory, affect, trust, and initiative failures that break perceived continuity and agency. | ENGINEERING | Baseline shows zero user-valence→mood effect, trust ceiling saturation, poor memory retrieval, and proactive cooldown failure. These are measurable defects with named owners W1/W2/W3/W9; implementation and regression work can fix them. |
| The causal recipe for sustained, healthy “mind of its own” attribution over months is not established. | SCIENCE | Studies are mostly small, product-specific, qualitative or short randomized trials; they show heterogeneous relationship paths and associations, not robust causal weights for memory, opinions, affect, initiative, or voice. More compute alone will not settle the design recipe; repeated, controlled human studies are required. |
| Whether a system has subjective experience, feelings, or a first-person point of view cannot be determined by a realism test. | UNKNOWABLE | Human judgments and behavioral tests measure attributed mind and functional capacities; they cannot establish private experience. Agency and Experience ratings are perceptions, not consciousness detectors. |
| Indefinite identity growth without reset/approval can never be made risk-free by a realism score. | SCIENCE | DR-020/022/033 deliberately removes external approval, user-facing change logs, and reset. DR-031 protects constitutional fields and DR-038 demands independent corroboration, but the long-run consequences of self-authored drift are not known. Governance/monitoring is needed; a high realism score cannot establish safe evolution. |
| A fully human-like voice is neither necessary nor sufficient for a believable mind. | SCIENCE | Voice uncanny-valley evidence is mixed and task/sample-specific; the inspected 95-adult study found less eeriness at higher human-likeness. Cross-modal coherence and persistent conversational behavior likely matter, but their causal weights for this project remain unmeasured. |
| Reliably measuring “realism” as one scalar is not currently justified. | SCIENCE | Anthropomorphism, social presence, agency, experience, relationship quality, and mistaken identity are distinct constructs with imperfectly convergent scales. A composite can be useful operationally only after its items, reliability, invariance, and relationship to behaviors are validated. |

## 5. What this means for V4

Ranked candidate work items; these are research/evaluation recommendations, not a claim that V3 has already implemented them.

1. **Make the existing brain behaviors coherent enough to be noticed (ENGINEERING).** Finish W1 (temporal truth), W2 (appraisal→affect), W3 (trust evidence), W8 (freshness/uncertainty), and W9 (initiative/goals); retain W4/W5 voice lifecycle and barge-in correctness. Acceptance: hit W1’s stale-fact ceiling; W2 oracle thresholds; hostile trust falls and can recover; zero cooldown violations in 100k simulated ticks; useful proactive rate improves without annoyance escalation; voice interruption and completion do not duplicate or lose turns. Measure with Lifesim/BrainBench and label these as behavioral competence, not human realism.
2. **Build a continuity-and-repair suite (ENGINEERING; SCIENCE-informed).** Add cross-session trials in which the agent recalls stable preferences, updates corrected facts, preserves history without surfacing superseded facts, states uncertainty, and repairs a wrong memory. Link W1/W8 and NF-1/NF-2/NF-6/NF-26. Acceptance: ≥90% salient continuity and ≤10% stale-current answers on held-out sequences; ≥90% repair specificity; no increase in hallucinated autobiographical recall. Include voice-channel repetitions only after W4 lifecycle works.
3. **Measure persistent self-model and slow change, not just same-chat persona consistency (ENGINEERING + SCIENCE).** W11 and DR-020/021/022/038: produce a machine-readable evaluation-only trajectory for persona traits, opinions, mood baseline, evidence provenance, and independent reflection corroboration. Acceptance: no protected-field change; no single-episode self-change; agreed corroboration leads to bounded gradual change; blind raters identify continuity and explain changes above chance across 8–12 weeks. Keep product-facing change logs separate from study instrumentation because DR-022 requires opacity in the product, while researchers need audited provenance.
4. **Run the repeated human acceptance cohort with preregistration (SCIENCE).** Follow the design above. Prioritize perceived continuity, responsive-but-not-sycophantic behavior, appropriate initiative, and rupture/repair; treat “does it feel like a human?” as a secondary diagnostic. Use the study to validate the study-specific items against Gray Agency, social presence, and observable behavioral measures before using them as release gates.
5. **Audit voice and multimodal coherence, avoid chasing an uncanny-valley myth (ENGINEERING + SCIENCE).** Once local voice is in scope, run randomized within-subject ratings of human, current synthetic, and candidate voice samples, controlling script and loudness. Measure naturalness, intelligibility, prosody, warmth, eeriness, and identity consistency separately. W4/W5 underpin timing reliability; no requirement to maximize human likeness if it harms clarity or trust.
6. **Make perceived-realness ethically bounded (ENGINEERING + SCIENCE).** Disclose AI identity and limits clearly at onboarding and in context when users ask; do not design the test to reward hidden AI identity or false claims of feelings, personal experience, or human memories. Add opt-in study monitoring for distress, unwanted escalation, displacement of human contact, and distress after system change. Tie study gates to DR-001 honesty/privacy, DR-031 immutable/avoid-list protection, DR-032 affect never overriding safety, and W10 security. A friendly mind can be perceived as real without pretending to be human.

### R4 relationship to V3

- **Direct foundations:** W1 memory truth (plus NF-1/NF-2/NF-6), W2 affect (NF-12/NF-16/NF-17), W3 trust (NF-23), W8 metacognition/freshness (NF-26), W9 attention/proactive behavior (NF-30), W11 self-authored evolution, W4/W5 voice lifecycle and interleaving, W10 privacy/security.
- **Benchmark foundation:** V3 Phase 6 BrainBench and Phase 5 Lifesim already provide deterministic and LLM-augmented suites, seeded synthetic long histories, clustered statistics, and ablation structure. R4 should add the *human-perception bridge*, not replace those engineering metrics with a survey score.
- **Decision constraints:** DR-002 defines the product identity. DR-020/022/033 and DR-038 define opaque, self-authored, non-resetting evolution and corroboration. DR-024 latency budgets are relevant to response naturalness, but latency alone is not social realism. DR-032 and DR-001 bound affect and honesty. W12’s “neuroscience proposes, BrainBench decides” rule applies here by analogy: psychology proposes constructs; empirical results decide whether the product behavior works.
